# Vehicle naming

Follow classic GTA San Andreas naming conventions for vehicle display names.
For ordinary cars, prefer short fictional word names without numeric engine-size
or model-year suffixes. The city car is named **Pip**. Numbered names exist in
stock SA (for example ZR-350, NRG-500 and AT-400), but use that style only when it fits
the vehicle category and the user's intent. Preserve existing internal asset
filenames and keys when changing a display name to avoid breaking installs.
